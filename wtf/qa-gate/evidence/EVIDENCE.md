## Boundary notes (honest limits)

- The two model metrics (`lip_sync_wer`, `face_sim`) are **stubbed in v1** by contract:
  they report `unavailable`, are excluded from the score, and are never fabricated.
  Live wiring hooks: `gate.LipSyncWERScorer(mode="live")` / `FaceSimScorer(mode="live")`
  (refused while dark). The WER math itself is implemented and tested (EN + Hindi).
- Black/silence/loudness numbers come from real ffmpeg detectors against real synthetic
  clips — not mocked.
- The shared production DB (`wtf/_data/factory.db`) is never touched by this package's
  tests or evidence; the demo DB lives inside `evidence/`.
